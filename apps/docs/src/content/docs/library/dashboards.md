## Arrange your overview

The overview on the library page and the Statistics page are both made of
widgets: the small number cards along the top and the panels below them. To
change what you see, choose **Customise** at the top of the page. Then:

- **Drag** a widget to move it, or use its arrows.
- **Choose a width** for a panel: a third, half, two thirds, or the whole row.
- **Tall** makes a panel two rows high, so two shorter panels stack beside it.
- **Your books** (library only) is a bar standing for your list of books.
  Move it like a widget: anything placed after it shows below your books
  rather than above them.
- **×** takes a widget off the page. Anything not on the page is listed at the
  top; choose it to add it back. Any widget can go on either page, so you
  could put Today's highlight on Statistics, or the weekday chart in your
  library.

To count something none of these widgets show, choose **New widget** and
[build your own](/widgets/custom-widgets/), or add a ready-made one from the
[Plugins](/widgets/plugins/) page. (Your own widgets are a desktop app
feature.)

Choose **Done** to keep your changes, or **Cancel** (or <kbd>Esc</kbd>) to
leave the page as it was. **Default layout** puts back the original
arrangement. Each page keeps its own layout. In the desktop app it's kept in
your settings file on this computer; in the [web app](/web/overview/) it's
kept with your account, so it follows you to any browser.

Some widgets aren't on either page to begin with. You can add them whenever you like:

- **On this day**: passages you highlighted on today's date in earlier years.
- **Currently reading**: the books you're reading, and when you last
  highlighted in each.
- **Pick back up**: paused books, plus books you're reading but haven't
  highlighted in a month.
- **Recently kept**: the last passages you highlighted.
- **Time of day**: the hours you tend to highlight.

On this day and Time of day need the time each highlight was made. Kobo,
Kindle and KOReader record it, but a pasted or typed-in passage has none. Choose
any passage in these widgets to open it on its book's page.
