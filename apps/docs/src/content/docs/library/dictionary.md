---
title: Offline dictionary
description: Look up English words without leaving your reading, connecting to the internet, or asking an AI.
---

A word should not take you away from a good passage. Leaflogue desktop includes
an English dictionary with definitions, parts of speech, and synonyms. The
WordNet 3.1 data ships with the app, so it works from the first launch without
a separate download, account, internet connection, or AI key.

## Define a word in a passage

1. Select a single word in a passage or reading note. Double-clicking a word
   is a quick way to select it.
2. Choose **Define “word”** beside your selection.
3. Read its meanings and synonyms in the dictionary window.
4. Press <kbd>Escape</kbd> or choose **Close dictionary** to return to reading.

The same lookup works on the book page, Highlights, and thematic threads.
Selecting text in an editing field does not show the lookup button.

## Search directly

Choose **Offline dictionary**, the book icon in the header, and enter a word
or short phrase. Press <kbd>Enter</kbd> or choose **Define**.

You can also press <kbd>⌘</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd> on Mac, or
<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd> on Linux, search for **offline dictionary**, and
choose **Open offline dictionary**.

Word case does not matter. Short phrases such as `free will` and `look up`
work too. Where possible, regular plurals and inflected forms resolve to a
base word. If there is no entry, try the singular or base form or check the
spelling. The dictionary may list several meanings; choose the one that fits
the passage rather than treating the first as the only meaning.

## What stays private

Lookup reads the bundled dictionary files on your computer. No selected word,
passage, or note is sent to a server. Definitions come from a dictionary;
they are not generated by an AI. Looking up a word does not change your
passages or add a note.

## Language and availability

The bundled dictionary is **English**, based on Princeton University's
[WordNet](https://wordnet.princeton.edu/). It is not a translation dictionary,
and it does not yet include pronunciation audio or downloadable language packs.
The [WordNet licence](/salon/WORDNET-LICENSE.txt) is also included in the app's
third-party notices.

This is a **desktop feature**. The web app and Libredex do not include the
bundled dictionary; use Leaflogue desktop for offline lookup.
