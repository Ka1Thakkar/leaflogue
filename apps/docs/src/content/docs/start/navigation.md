## Search and commands

The icons down the left side open Library, Highlights, Threads, Statistics,
Import, and Settings. In a narrow window they move to a bar along the bottom.

The quickest way to get anywhere is the search bar at the top of every page.
Click it, or press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> on
Windows and Linux), and start typing. One list finds:

- **Books**, by title, author, or series.
- **Passages and notes** that contain the words you type.
- **Tags, threads, and collections.** A tag opens its passages; a collection
  opens its books.
- **Pages**, such as Statistics or Import.
- **Every setting.** Choose one and Settings opens with that control ready to
  change.
- **Actions:** import from a plugged-in e-reader, add a book, export, make a
  backup, check for updates, change the theme or switch between light and
  dark, and expand or fold the sidebar. In the
  [web app](/web/overview/) the list leaves out what needs files on your
  computer (exporting, backups, updates, plugged-in readers) and adds
  **Sign out**.

The page you're on adds its own actions too. On a book's page you can mark it
finished, add a highlight, edit its details, or add it to a collection; on the
library and Statistics pages, **Customise this page**. If you've turned on
[AI](/ai/gemini/), a passage you've selected offers **Explain this passage**.

Before you type anything, the list starts with what you opened or ran lately.
To narrow it, start with one of these:

| Type | To see only |
| --- | --- |
| `>` | actions |
| `#` | tags |
| `@` | books by an author |
| `/` | pages and settings |

The last row always searches all your highlights for the words you typed. Your
searches and recent choices stay on this computer (or in this browser).

The sidebar starts as a column of icons. To see their names as well, choose
the sidebar button at the top of the sidebar, or press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>B</kbd> (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>B</kbd> on
Windows and Linux). Drag the sidebar's right edge to make it wider or
narrower; drag it most of the way closed, or double-click the edge, to fold
it back to icons. Leaflogue remembers your choice.

## Reader and Assistant panel

In 0.1.1, the header's rightmost button opens or closes a
right-hand context panel with **Reader** and **Assistant** tabs. **Read** on
a desktop book opens the Reader; **Explain this passage** opens the Assistant.
Drag the panel's left edge to resize it. Its expand control fills the workspace;
restore split view to see the book page beside it. Narrow windows show the
panel across the available workspace.

Switching tabs or closing the panel keeps the current session in that window.
Reader position is saved across restarts; assistant conversations are not.
Use Left/Right or Home/End while a tab is focused to switch tabs. Reader page
controls are listed in [Read an EPUB](/library/epub/#turn-pages-and-change-the-display).

## Keyboard shortcuts

| Press | To |
| --- | --- |
| <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd>) | Open or close search and commands |
| <kbd>↑</kbd> <kbd>↓</kbd> | Move through the results |
| <kbd>Enter</kbd> | Open the chosen result |
| <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>Enter</kbd> (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>Enter</kbd>) | Its second choice, where the bottom of the list shows one, such as copying a passage |
| <kbd>Esc</kbd> | Close search, or a dialog |
| <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>B</kbd> (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>B</kbd>) | Expand or fold the sidebar |
