## 1. Choose where to keep your library

The first time Leaflogue opens, setup shows where it will keep your library
and settings. Choose **Local only** to start without an account or database.
Your books, passages, and notes stay in a SQLite file on this computer.

You can add [Convex or PostgreSQL sync](/sync/overview/) later in Settings.
You do not need sync to import, read, edit, search, or back up your library.

## 2. Bring in your books

Choose **Import** on the library page. Pick your e-reader's highlights file,
review what Leaflogue found, name the source, and confirm the import. The
[importing guide](/library/importing/) shows where each reader keeps its files.

To start with a paper book instead, choose **Add book**, enter its title and
author, and open the book to add your first highlight.

## 3. Keep a thought beside a passage

Open a book and point at a passage, or move to its controls with <kbd>Tab</kbd>.
Choose **Add another note** to write a Markdown note. Passages keep the book's
words as plain text; notes support formatting and links.

Use **Manage tags** to label a passage or **Add to thematic thread** to connect
it to passages from other books. See [Read and organise](/library/reading/).

## 4. Find it again

Click the search bar or press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd> on Linux
(<kbd>⌘</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd> on a Mac). Type a title, an author, a phrase, or the
name of an action. Choosing the last search row opens Highlights with all
matching passages and notes.

[Search and keyboard shortcuts](/start/navigation/) explains the command
palette, sidebar, and keyboard controls.

## 5. Make a backup

Open **Settings → Backups and your data** and choose **Create backup**.
Save it somewhere you can recover if this computer fails. A backup contains
your library; it does not contain your saved keys or settings.

[Back up and restore](/library/backups/) explains how to check and restore it.

## Make the space yours

- Choose a theme and light, dark, or system appearance in **Settings**.
- Choose **Customise** on Library or Statistics to
  [arrange your dashboards](/library/dashboards/).
- Add optional [AI explanations](/ai/gemini/) or [sync](/sync/overview/)
  when you want them. Both require your own provider or service configuration.
