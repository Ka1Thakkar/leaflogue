---
title: Review synced changes
description: What Leaflogue does when two computers change the same thing.
---

When two computers change the same passage or note before syncing, Leaflogue
cannot know which you meant. It never throws either away:

- **One version wins on every computer**, so all your computers show the
  same library.
- **The other version is kept** on the computer where it was written, waiting
  for you to choose.
- **A deletion wins over an edit.** If one computer deleted a passage that the
  other edited, it is deleted everywhere and the edit is kept for review.

Waiting reviews appear in the notification bell. Open **Review synced
changes** to see each one side by side, **In your library now** next to
**Version that was not kept**, then choose:

- **Restore this version** to bring the kept version back into your library.
  A restored deletion comes back as a new passage.
- **Discard this version** if the version in your library is the one you want.

Reviews stay until you decide, and they never stop syncing.
