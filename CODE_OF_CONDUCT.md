# Code of conduct

Leaflogue is built by and for people who care about reading. We want every
contributor, reviewer, and user to be able to take part without harassment or
hostility, whatever their background, identity, or experience.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, and assume good intent.
- Give and receive feedback about work, not people.
- Respect differing opinions and the time of volunteers.
- Keep other people's reading data, notes, and personal details private.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation, threats, or discriminatory language or imagery.
- Personal attacks, insults, or deliberate disruption of discussions.
- Publishing someone else's private information without permission.
- Sexualised language or attention, or other conduct inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies in every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions, and
any channel that represents the project. Report concerns privately to the
maintainer through the contact on the maintainer's GitHub profile, or by using
GitHub's report feature. Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may
remove content, and temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour
breaks this code, with the response proportionate to the harm.
